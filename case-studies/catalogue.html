Coles Digital Catalogue Animation

Embedding motion into Coles' digital catalogue experience to increase engagement and bring promotional content to life.

Overview

Coles' digital catalogue experience was primarily static despite increasing customer engagement with digital channels. I introduced and scaled motion-led executions across major seasonal campaigns, establishing animation as a repeatable and measurable part of the catalogue experience.

Motion was delivered as MP4 catalogue animations embedded into the Coles website.

Results

28.8% Click-through-rate uplift across key seasonal campaigns
Weekly Motion delivered across ongoing catalogue executions
Established Animation as a repeatable digital catalogue capability

Challenge

Coles' digital catalogue experience was primarily static, limiting opportunities to use motion to increase engagement and bring promotional content to life.

Approach

I introduced motion-led executions across major seasonal campaigns and developed a repeatable production approach that allowed animation to become part of the ongoing catalogue workflow.

Outcome

Animation became an ongoing part of the digital catalogue experience, creating a scalable way to introduce motion into promotional content while supporting measurable engagement improvements.

Motion became a repeatable part of the catalogue experience, rather than a one-off campaign treatment.
